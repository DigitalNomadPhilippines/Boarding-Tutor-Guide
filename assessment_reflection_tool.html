Assessment and Reflection Tool Instructions

This tool is designed to help boarding tutors assess their performance and reflect on their practice. Complete this form regularly (quarterly or termly) to track your professional growth and identify areas for development.

Important: Be honest in your self-assessment. This tool is primarily for your own professional development, though you may choose to share it with supervisors during performance reviews.

Boarding Tutor Self-Assessment and Reflection Tool

Basic Information

Competency Self-Assessment

Rate your current level of competency in each of the following areas:

1. Student Relationship Building

Reflect on how you build and maintain positive relationships with students. What strategies have been most effective?

2. Academic Support and Guidance

Consider your ability to support students academically. How do you help students with their studies and academic challenges?

3. Pastoral Care and Emotional Support

Reflect on how you provide emotional support and pastoral care to students. How do you identify and address wellbeing concerns?

4. Behavior Management

Consider your approach to managing student behavior. How effective are your strategies for maintaining discipline while building respect?

5. Communication with Parents/Guardians

Reflect on your communication with parents and guardians. How do you build and maintain these important relationships?

6. Collaboration with Colleagues

Consider how you work with other staff members. How do you contribute to the boarding team?

7. Activity Planning and Implementation

Reflect on your ability to plan and implement engaging activities for boarding students. What types of activities have been most successful?

8. Cultural Sensitivity and Inclusion

Consider your approach to cultural diversity and inclusion. How do you ensure all students feel respected and included?

9. Crisis Management

Reflect on your ability to handle emergencies or crises. How prepared do you feel to respond to unexpected situations?

10. Professional Development

Consider your commitment to ongoing learning and improvement. How do you pursue professional growth?
