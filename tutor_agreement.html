3. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The Tutor agrees to:

  1. Provide academic support and pastoral care to boarding students as assigned
  2. Supervise boarding house activities during assigned duty hours
  3. Maintain a safe and supportive environment for all students
  4. Enforce school rules and boarding house policies consistently
  5. Communicate regularly with house staff, academic staff, and parents as appropriate
  6. Attend staff meetings and professional development sessions as required
  7. Complete all required documentation and record-keeping
  8. Report any concerns regarding student welfare promptly
  9. Participate in boarding house activities and events
  10. Perform other reasonable duties as assigned by the Head of Boarding

6. ACCOMMODATION (if applicable)

The School will provide the Tutor with accommodation in the boarding house, subject to the following conditions:

  • The accommodation is provided for the duration of this agreement only
  • The Tutor must maintain the accommodation in good condition
  • The Tutor must comply with all school policies regarding on-campus accommodation
  • The School reserves the right to inspect the accommodation with reasonable notice
  • The Tutor is responsible for personal property insurance
7. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Tutor agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all student information, school records, and other sensitive information accessed during the course of employment. This obligation continues after the termination of this agreement.

8. CHILD PROTECTION AND SAFEGUARDING

The Tutor acknowledges receipt of the School's Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy and agrees to comply with all requirements, including:

  • Completing required background checks
  • Participating in safeguarding training
  • Reporting any concerns according to school protocols
  • Maintaining appropriate professional boundaries with students
