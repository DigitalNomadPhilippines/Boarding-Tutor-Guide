Professional Organizations

These organizations provide valuable resources, networking opportunities, and professional development for boarding tutors and residential educators.

International Organizations

  • The Boarding Schools' Association (BSA)

    The leading organization for boarding education in the UK and internationally, offering training, conferences, publications, and professional development opportunities.

    www.boarding.org.uk

  • The Association of Boarding Schools (TABS)

    North American organization supporting boarding schools through research, professional development, and advocacy.

    www.boardingschools.com

  • Australian Boarding Schools Association (ABSA)

    Represents boarding schools across Australia, providing resources, training, and standards for boarding practice.

    www.boarding.org.au

  • International Coalition for Boarding School Reform (ICBSR)

    Global network focused on best practices and innovation in residential education.

    www.icbsr.org

Specialized Organizations

  • National Association of Independent Schools (NAIS)

    Provides resources on independent education, including boarding programs.

    www.nais.org

  • International School Counselor Association (ISCA)

    Support network for counselors in international schools, including boarding settings.

    www.iscainfo.com

  • Association for Experiential Education (AEE)

    Resources for experiential learning programs often used in boarding settings.

    www.aee.org

  • International Association for College Admission Counseling (IACAC)

    Resources for supporting international students in college preparation.

    www.iacac.org

Books and Publications

These publications provide in-depth knowledge and practical guidance for boarding tutors.

Boarding Education

  • The Boarding School Survival Guide by Justin Ross Muchnick

    Insights into the boarding school experience from a student perspective.

  • Boarding Schools at the Crossroads of Change by Pearl Rock Kane

    Analysis of trends and developments in boarding education.

  • A Home Away from Home: Boarding School Pastoral Care by David Anderson

    Comprehensive guide to pastoral care in residential settings.

  • International Education in Practice edited by Mary Hayden, Jeff Thompson, and George Walker

    Perspectives on international boarding education.

Adolescent Development and Support

  • The Teenage Brain by Frances E. Jensen

    Neuroscience insights into adolescent development and behavior.

  • Brainstorm: The Power and Purpose of the Teenage Brain by Daniel J. Siegel

    Understanding adolescent development and supporting healthy growth.

  • The Growth Mindset Coach by Annie Brock and Heather Hundley

    Practical strategies for fostering resilience and growth mindset.

  • Culturally Responsive Teaching and The Brain by Zaretta Hammond

    Strategies for supporting diverse student populations.

Journals and Periodicals

  • Boarding School Magazine (BSA)

    Regular publication with articles on boarding education practice.

  • International Journal of Boarding Education

    Research and best practices in global boarding contexts.

  • Journal of Residential Education

    Academic research on residential learning communities.

  • Independent School Magazine (NAIS)

    Articles on independent education including boarding programs.
