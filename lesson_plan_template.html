Tips for Effective Lesson Planning:

  • Align objectives with curriculum requirements and student needs
  • Plan for a variety of activities to maintain engagement
  • Include both individual and collaborative learning opportunities when appropriate
  • Build in regular checks for understanding
  • Allow flexibility to respond to student needs during the session
  • Consider the pace and timing of activities
  • Plan meaningful homework that reinforces learning
  • Complete the reflection section as soon as possible after the session
